## 2. Chapter alignment (from the main session; memo itself not in repo)

- Sociotechnical imaginaries = the discursive layer of the PI's empathy *dispositif*; the products are its material layer. No need to choose between the two frames.
- Each product imagines autism and AI together, as mirror images:

| Product | Autism imagined as | AI imagined as |
|---|---|---|
| StellarCare (CAS) | early-detectable signal (eye movement, micro-expressions) | **detector** that sees earlier than humans |
| ALSOLIFE 个训机器人 | quantifiable deficit to be trained ("broken computer") | tireless **trainer**, "3× human efficiency" |
| 追星星的AI | distant star to be reached; must learn 心智解读 | "warm" **understander** |

  One-line thesis: these products take the capacity to "understand others" away from autistic people and assign it to machines.
- Chiasmus examples: "人有温度AI才有温度"; ZX-V2 narrated in the AI's first person ("我终于学会了和星星世界沟通的方法"); AL-V4 "困在机器人里的灵魂".
- Counterpoint: neurodivergent appropriation of general-purpose chatbots; 小红书 post: "既不是安慰，也不是共情，而是一种罕见的『思维对齐』".
- Cross-chapter hooks: micro-expression screening → Ch. 2 (facial coding); Kaspar/QTrobot in Chinese reports → Ch. 4; ND appropriation → Ch. 5.
- Draft English abstract to replace the PI's old Ch. 3 summary:

> Chapter 3 turns to China, where the generative-AI boom has produced a wave of AI products for autistic children, from multimodal early-screening "agents" developed by state research institutes to home-rehabilitation "robots" and platform-philanthropy picture-book generators. Reading these products as sociotechnical imaginaries, the chapter shows how each imagines autism and AI together: autism as a signal to be detected early, a developmental deficit to be trained and scored, or a distant "star" to be reached; and AI, correspondingly, as detector, tireless trainer, or "warm" understander. Set against these products designed for autism, neurodivergent people's own uses of general-purpose chatbots (as mouthpiece, thinking partner, or scaffold for action) describe the relation not as empathy but as "alignment", unsettling the criterion on which the products are built.

Literature mentioned so far (all **unverified**): Jasanoff & Kim; Milton (double empathy); Akrich, Woolgar, Spiel (imagined user); Kafer; Shew; Baron-Cohen; Murray; Yergeau.

## 3. ALSOLIFE analysis (condensed; chat only until now)

Overall: autism as a **measurable, decomposable developmental deficit fillable by algorithm**; its main consequence framed as **family burden**; goal is normalisation; autistic people themselves absent.

1. **Broken machine**: founder 张之光 in 《中国慈善家》: autistic child "像是键盘和显卡出现故障的电脑"; "隔着一堵很厚的墙", resolve to "突破这面墙". AL-V4 robot-soul metaphor matches (not clearly ALSOLIFE's own video). Irony: autism as machine fault, repaired by a "robot".
2. **Quantified lag**: "精准测量孩子的发育基线""发育商""174 项里程碑技能""查缺补漏，填补每个能力缺口"; daily/weekly reports to parents; offline centre ALSO·IN data "反哺算法" (child as data source). Blurs autism with 发育迟缓 (Taobao title and Q&A).
3. **Medicalisation**: "数字疗法""医疗级实证"; Peking Univ. Sixth Hospital claim "干预效率是传统人工的 3 倍""P<0.05". **Check**: website figure for VBMAPP shows **P=0.055** while text says P<0.05; original paper not yet checked.
4. **Adaptation/compliance**: "训练认知和行为规范…适应社会"; success = readmitted to kindergarten; entry threshold "安坐 5 分钟以上""能配合""较少的问题行为"; ABA-style reinforcement; reviews praise sitting still and eagerness to study.
5. **Slow learner / patient machine**: "机器没脾气，足够耐心"; dynamic images vs static cards ("原来这就是跳啊！").
6. **Family burden**: slogan "给每个孤独症孩子的家长，构建每天一小时的喘息时间"; AL-V5 ending (father with beer, "家长也真正意义上有了属于自己的时间和空间"); 秋爸爸 video uses 关键期 urgency, his 22-year-old son as cautionary case; founder's lifelong-dependence arc (康复 → 情感陪伴 → 养护), "执子之手，历一世劫".
7. **Tech-efficiency solutionism**: "降本增效", 1/10–1/20 of human cost; "有理工科背景的爸爸"; grouped with FDA-approved ADHD game, BrainCo. "机器人" naming though it is "像是一个学习机".
8. **Critics' counter-imaginary (autism as relational)**: 静姐 (AL-V1): "AI 就是很机械的在训练他…没有任何的感情色彩"; 杨老师 (AL-V3): needs "人和人之间的互动", teachers say "机器人取代不了""走上了另一条歪路", "把程序和机器人等同起来". Company reply: max 2 lessons/day, "剩下 23 小时还是要依赖人".
9. **Absences**: no autistic voice, no adults, no neurodiversity framing; puzzle-piece lightbulb in the heic image.

Source cautions: 《中国慈善家》 piece reads as company narrative; some Taobao reviews look templated/fake.

## 4. 其他产品 analysis: StellarCare + 启音 (condensed; chat only until now)

Overall: autism as a **childhood defect to be caught early, measured and corrected; technology "lights up" and "awakens" the child; autistic people never speak.**

Material notes: the 4 StellarCare HTML pages are reprints of **one press release** (not independent); the StellarCare video is a 自媒体 explainer ("老吴讲数字化"); 启音 looks like a design concept/portfolio (placeholder data dated 2028); CyberDaily article is paywall-truncated (duplicate of the how-AI-help-ASD copy).

1. **Pathology; "neurodiversity" hollowed out**: CyberDaily "AI治愈自闭症、多动症、PTSD等神经多样性群体"; press release "神经发育障碍性疾病""位居青少年精神残疾首位"; 启音 UI "治疗模式""准备好开启治疗了吗".
2. **Race against time**: "黄金干预期", diagnosis moved "提前至18月龄", "筛查难、确诊晚、干预迟"; autism ≈ childhood, though "超1300万孤独症患者" are mostly adults.

5. **Users are parents, doctors, state**: StellarCare scenarios 家庭/医疗/社会; 启音 account types only "治疗师 / 家长 / 专业医生"; child's consent = "画一个笑脸告诉我们你已经准备好了".

## 5. Neurodivergent people's own AI use (report on its branch)

Full report: `reports/神经多元人士的创造性AI使用与主流产品想象.md` on `神经多元人士的自主AI使用`. Based on the 4 items on that branch (理想者大会 minutes and transcripts, a Hong Kong parent's 小红书 post, the "思维对齐" 小红书 post and comments, the 青衫Neuro ADHD article), compared with the product cases.

Core contrast: products design AI **for** autism (detect, train, read the child; real users are parents, doctors, the state). The community **appropriates general-purpose AI** (豆包, 即梦, 飞书妙搭, 扣子) to express, claim rights, create, get started, and "translate" the world. Products make the child fit the world; the community uses AI to make the world fit the person.

Six uses: (1) mouthpiece, up to rights claims (冯东's daughter's 《全球全员公约》, complaints posted in a family group); (2) echolalia answered patiently and differently each time (云霄, "我想五姨奶了"); (3) special interests into creation (波波, 28, 即梦 → 3D-printed charm); (4) **community-built tools** (爱纳's teacher-voice agent and apps; 上海慧灵's local "托付" skill and one-keystroke easy-read converter; ByteDance volunteers' tools), a new category for the coding scheme; (5) self-directed cognitive scaffolds (ADHD author: "问题不是我不爱学习，而是学习方式不匹配"); (6) "alignment" not empathy ("ai不会觉得你怪""不会脑补""不耗能").

Four differences from the products: who understands whom (gap placed between both sides, a lived double-empathy problem: "是我们没有能力翻译出来"); "symptoms" become resources; children and the golden period vs adults and a whole life (employment, sex, 托付, death; 冯东 dropped a digital-twin plan as possibly "阴魂不散"); AI valued precisely because it does **not** empathise, while "温度" stays in relationships ("蒸馏不出一个关系", 张真).

Not to romanticise: no autistic speaker on the forum stage (uses are reported second-hand); 豆包's agreeableness both helps and fed 冯东's daughter's belief that the earth "restarts" on 1 Sept; protection vs empowerment ("心智障碍者模式" vs "数字阉割"); platforms "distilling" NGO expertise; an audience member's queer critique.

To decide/verify: whether the forum and speakers can be named, and name variants (张真/张臻/张珍, 陈荣东/陈文东); correct 冯东's daughter's pronoun (transcript says 他); the "14-year-old Asperger's boy" case (guessed Character.AI lawsuit, unverified).
